Delete your account and data

Last updated: 29 August 2026

You can delete your Nestlog account and everything stored on our server at any time. The fastest route is inside the app, and it takes effect immediately.

In the app — the quickest way

  1. Open Nestlog.
  2. Tap the settings icon, at the top right of the Home screen.
  3. Scroll to Account.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion happens straight away and cannot be undone.

If you cannot open the app

Lost the phone, or already uninstalled? Email us from the address you signed up with — we can only act on a request that comes from that address, since it is the only way to confirm the account is yours.

Email a deletion request

We will action it within 30 days, and normally much sooner. You will get a reply confirming when it is done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in credentials.
  • Your membership of any household.
  • Your notification token, so your devices stop being sent sync signals.
  • Every entry in a household where you are the only member — feeds, sleep, nappies, growth, pumping, medicine, temperature and notes.

What does not get deleted, and why

If a second parent shares your household, the household and its entries survive. Their copy of the log is not yours to destroy, and deleting your account would otherwise wipe the record of their baby from their phone too. Your membership is removed, and the entries you logged stop being attributed to you.

If you are the only member, there is nothing to preserve and everything goes.

The log on your own phone is separate from the server copy. Deleting the account removes the server data; to clear the phone as well, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android Settings.

Your Pro unlock stays with you. It belongs to your Google Play account rather than your Nestlog account, so deleting the latter does not cost you the purchase, and you will not be shown the paywall again if you come back.

Google Play purchase records are held by Google, not by us, and are outside what we can delete. Google's retention applies.

Want a copy first?

Deletion is permanent, so export before you go if you want to keep the record: Settings → Export as CSV gives you every entry as a spreadsheet, free, and works without an account.
